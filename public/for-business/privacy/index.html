Business Privacy Notice

Effective 12 October 2026. Contact: SavePulse operator, the contact address in our main privacy policy.

What we collect and why

When you request an exploratory demo, we collect your company, country, business email, position, team, area of interest, problem description and consent record. We use these details to respond, evaluate fit and manage your requested conversation. We rely on your consent for this follow-up. We do not subscribe you to unrelated marketing.

Calculator assumptions are processed locally in your browser and are not included in this form. Do not submit confidential documents, financial amounts, sensitive information or data about other people.

Processors and storage

Netlify handles and stores form submissions and may process technical metadata for security and spam prevention. Our restricted Google Sheets CRM and Gmail may hold the request and correspondence when the follow-up connection is activated. These providers may process data outside your country. We limit access to those handling your request; we do not sell your contact details.

Retention

We review inactive research enquiries after 12 months and remove personal details when no longer needed. Correspondence needed for an ongoing relationship or legal obligation may be retained longer. Minimal suppression records may be kept to honour requests not to contact you.

Your choices

Email the contact address in our main privacy policy to withdraw consent, stop follow-up, or request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, subject to applicable law. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. You may also contact your relevant data-protection authority.

This notice supplements the main SavePulse privacy policy. It describes research enquiries and does not represent a security certification.
