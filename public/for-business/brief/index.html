SavePulse for Business · Executive research brief

Know your procurement exposure before payment.

For CFOs, Finance Directors and Procurement Directors.

The question

Can your team explain the reporting-currency cost change on approved-but-unpaid purchase orders, separating supplier-price changes from FX changes?

What the current tool does

A browser calculator compares quantity × supplier unit price × manually entered exchange rate, plus fixed local costs. It separates the supplier-price effect at the budgeted FX rate from the FX effect at the updated price, with an assumed locked FX share. It creates a printable scenario summary.

Illustrative example

100 units, supplier price 100→110, FX 35→36 THB per supplier-currency unit, no locked FX or fixed costs: approved budget THB 350,000; scenario THB 396,000; variance THB 46,000 (price THB 35,000 + FX THB 11,000). This is arithmetic under assumptions, not a forecast or savings result.

Limits

Early-stage research. No real-time market feed, commercial TradingView licence claim, ERP integration, hedging recommendation, measured savings or enterprise security certification. Contract terms, fees and hedge mark-to-market are outside this simplified scenario.
