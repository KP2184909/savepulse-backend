## Objective and current product boundary
500 unique legal companies with actual payment evidence and active subscription access. A prospect, executive name, sent email, auto-reply, demo request or pilot interest does not count. Existing B2B capability is a manually entered scenario calculator and printable summary. There is no proven cash-saving outcome, live commercial TradingView feed, ERP integration or enterprise certification claim.

## Account selection before geographic scale
Research every market without a six-country restriction. Require an English-language business route at the company level rather than inferring buying suitability from a country's official language. The CRM inventory includes 249 market/territory codes from a 2023 OS ISO snapshot; it is a research queue, not 249 verified English markets. Dependencies and territories are not all sovereign countries. Do not claim exhaustive live country/language verification.

Prioritise chemical, ingredient, polymer, metal and commodity importers with repeated foreign-currency purchases. Interview for: open unpaid POs; variable supplier prices; meaningful currency mismatch; a gap in the current spreadsheet/ERP review; an accountable buyer; and willingness to evaluate a defined pilot. These are qualification requirements, not established facts about researched accounts.

Deprioritise domestic USD-only purchasing, fully covered exposures, infrequent imports, mature processes with no stated gap, and enterprise buyers whose security/integration requirements the current product cannot meet.

## Executive-first, with a practical buying path
First identify the owner/CEO/Managing Director or controlling executive and verify the current role on an official company page or filing. Record chair/owner separately from the operating CEO when different. Formal board seniority does not prove approval authority for a small software subscription.
- Owner-led distributor: owner/MD is likely the best initial sponsor; verify actual approval authority.
- Larger firm: identify top executive first, then CFO/Finance Director for the economic case and Treasury/Procurement/Controller for the workflow.
- Never invent an executive email from a naming pattern. A public sales/general inbox is a routing channel, not a verified CEO mailbox.
- Do not send to IR, whistleblowing, emergency, credit-control or accounts-payable addresses simply because they are public.
- One company-level outreach record. Do not concurrently message every department. Country offices are not automatically separate paying legal companies.

## Per-market release gates
- UK: distinguish corporate subscriber from individual subscriber; use accurate sender identity, opt-out, suppression and appropriate personal-data basis. Cornelius Group PLC's active legal entity is confirmed by Companies House: https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/01936460
- USA: CAN-SPAM applies to B2B; accurate headers/subject, disclosure, valid physical postal address, working opt-out and suppression. No approved sender postal address is assumed.
- Australia: document consent basis before marketing; accurate sender details and unsubscribe. Published addresses are not blanket permission.
- Canada: document CASL consent/basis, identity and unsubscribe; sending from another country does not remove the recipient-country gate.
- NZ: assess consent, sender identity, unsubscribe and anti-harvesting requirements.
- South Africa: assess POPIA section 69 and consent basis; no automatic cold send released.
- Ireland, Singapore and all other markets: record the applicable rules and recipient-specific basis before release. Some primary guidance pages could only be retrieved via search index or were blocked, so no complete legal review is claimed.
- All: recheck original CRM, Gmail history and suppression immediately before send. Never guess addresses or invent measured savings. Founder approval remains required for the outbound batch, pricing, contracts and costs.
