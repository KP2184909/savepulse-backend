# SavePulse for Business — Executive Demand Generation Kit
Version: 2026-10-12. Status: customer-discovery assets; not evidence of live B2B product capabilities.

## Audience and offer
- Chief Financial Officer / Finance Director: Approved PO vs updated reporting-currency cost, by supplier and payment date.
- Financial Controller / Finance Manager: budget-vs-current tracking, manual reconciliation, and audit-friendly explanations.
- Procurement Director: supplier price movement versus currency movement in expected landed cost.
- CEO / owner of importer/distributor: visibility into gross-margin risk before invoicing customers.

**Primary promise**: "Know your procurement exposure before payment." This is a proposed information/visibility benefit, **not** a promise to save cash, hedge FX, automate ERP integrations, or provide a live commodity-price feed.

**Landing page on feature branch**: https://savepulse.cloud/for-business/ (ONLY after production deploy).
**No-signup calculator**: quantity × unit price × FX; supports locked FX share by assumption, fixed other costs, supplier price effect, FX effect, budget variance, and printable executive brief.
**Source rules**: all on-screen currency/commodity prices are manual user entries or clearly illustrative samples; never imply live or licensed TradingView data. Live market data needs appropriate licenses and legal review.

## Website hero / executive hook
Your supplier quote stayed the same.
Did your landed cost?

A foreign-currency purchase order can cost more in your reporting currency before payment. Compare the price, the exchange rate and the approved budget in a transparent illustrative scenario.

CTA: "Run the free exposure calculator". Secondary: "Ask for a 10-minute research conversation".
Proof statement: "Early-stage research. No claimed or guaranteed savings."

## Partner research approach
Prioritise accounting firms, procurement consultancies, trade associations, customs and importing advisers, ERP implementers and manufacturing controllers. Offer a co-branded *illustrative* calculator, not a commission guarantee.
Partner discovery angle: "Do your importer clients struggle to reconcile foreign-currency POs to budgeted landed costs?"

## 30-day distribution experiment
Week 1: Publish B2B landing, test mobile/print and calculations, QA disclaimers and lead email. Do not promote a broken page.
Week 2: Publish Post A and a 70s video; share with 10 opt-in/warm finance professionals or verified company channels, recording clicks and human replies.
Week 3: Publish Posts B/C; research 20 partner organisations; prepare compliant, personalised introductions; hold discovery calls.
Week 4: Review country/persona-specific replies, demo requests, pilot interest and bounce complaints in the Global Growth CRM; change positioning before increasing volume.

Success criteria (hypotheses, not benchmarks): 10+ qualified calculator completions or substantive reactions, 5 discovery conversations, at least one specifically defined paid-pilot willingness signal. Track actual subscriptions only from billing evidence.

## Executive objection guide
"Is this financial advice?" No: illustrative procurement cost variance, not predictions or hedge advice.
"Does it integrate with SAP / Oracle / Odoo?" Not claimed. Ask which systems and exports matter; log research requirements.
"Are those live prices?" No, they are user-entered assumptions; future commercial market feeds need appropriate licenses.
"Does it save money?" We have not measured savings; value proposition is cost visibility / faster review, to be validated.
"Are you SOC 2 / ISO 27001 compliant?" Do not claim certifications; ask procurement for security requirements and founder approval.
"Can we start a pilot?" Capture use case, timing, sensitivity and requirements; submit proposed scope/pricing for founder approval.
